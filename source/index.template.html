A THEME FOR T3 CODE
Washi

Ink spreads while your agent works. A seal is pressed when it's done.

A calm, light theme: fibrous paper, sumi ink and one vermilion accent. Nothing moves at rest. While a turn runs, ink spreads in the margin, a brush line is drawn under the header, and the page waits for its seal.

WHAT MOVES, AND WHY

Stillness is the default.

Every animation is tied to real app state. A quiet screen is a finished page; motion means the agent is working. Only transform, opacity and background position move, so it stays light on the machine.

WORKING

Ink bloom

In the left margin a drop of sumi spreads on wet paper over about nine seconds: a dark core and a tide-line ring. Over the next half minute three satellite stains wick out of it and the whole bloom creeps outward for a couple of minutes, so a long turn keeps showing wet ink and never resets. At rest it is a faint, dry stain.

WORKING

Brush lines

A stroke is drawn once along the header and under the live Working row, then stays put until the turn ends. Nothing loops or blinks.

TURN COMPLETE

Hanko seal

While a turn runs the vermilion seal is lifted off the page. When the working row goes away it drops back and is pressed with a small bounce. This is the one-shot beat that says done.

BACKGROUND THREAD

Drifting wash

A thread working in the background gets a pale wash that drifts slowly across its sidebar row.

FOCUS & HOVER

Indigo and sumi strokes

Focus the composer and a line of indigo ink runs under it. Hover a thread and a brush stroke trails beneath the row.

AT REST

Bamboo and a brushed ring

Bamboo in the left margin and a hand-brushed enso on the right stay perfectly still. They are painted into the paper, so they cost nothing to draw.

IdleBamboo, an open ring, a pale stain and the seal. Dead still.

PALETTE

INSTALL

Three ways in.

Washi is a single JSON file. No images, no fonts to install.

一

Paste it in

Copy the theme, then in T3 Code open Settings › Appearance › Add theme and paste.

二

Download

Save washi.json and choose it from the same Add theme dialog.

Download washi.json
三

Terminal

Fetch and set it from the command line.

curl -fsSL -o washi.json https://joshsaintjacque.github.io/t3code-theme-washi/washi.json
~/.t3/bin/t3 theme set washi.json
